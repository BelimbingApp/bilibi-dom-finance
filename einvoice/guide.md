# Source-linked fiscal documents

Read the source through Receivables or Payables and obtain its posted entry ID. Supply a fiscal draft with the counterparty and lines, plus `source: {module, record, ledger_entry_id}` (`record` is the ledger owner's reference, for example `document:<id>` or `payment:<id>`). The source must be owned by `finance/receivables` or `finance/payables`, unreversed, in the document currency and for its total. This validates accounting recognition, not record kind, line-by-line or party equivalence. The company's own party and environment come from its e-invoice settings.

Use `finance.einvoice.document.issue` through a reviewed plan, never direct call. Send decimal operands as strings, `issued_at` as an explicit whole-second ISO instant with offset (not future), and one to 1,000 lines. `01` is an ordinary invoice with `buyer`; `11` is a self-billed invoice with `supplier` including industry code and activity. Notes require a valid or rejected original invoice with an authority UUID and their own correcting source. Read company-adjusted authority codes through the normal e-invoice facade; the current authority supports MYR only. Configure the profile in settings before issuing.

Review the resolved environment, both party snapshots, normalized lines, derived amounts, details and source. Ask the person for consent before commit. The plan code proves the reviewed contract, not consent. Changed facts require a new plan; do not silently change the draft or its source. Dependent steps may require separate plans. Commit shares one audited transaction; a later failure rolls all earlier writes back. A repeated commit code is refused.

Issuing records an immutable `issued` document and source link only. It neither submits to MyInvois nor posts a second entry. Submission, refresh, cancellation, consolidation and PDF generation are not H4 operations. Accounting corrections remain with the source module. Two merely issued documents may link one source; a standing document or unresolved submission reserves it.

Use `.get` with company/document IDs for full document details and `.list` for summaries newest first. Pass `next_before` as `before` until it is null; default page size 50, maximum 100. Reads require `finance.einvoice.view`; issuance requires `finance.einvoice.documents.issue` and `finance.ledger.view`, not e-invoice view. Tenant and actor always come from the credential. Never send actor, tenant, totals, environment, secrets or approval identities. No new pages are supplied by these operations.
